# Road to Glory policy review

The Vietnamese and English drafts are available at `/privacy` and `/terms`. They are marked as review drafts and carry a noindex directive. The landing footer links to both. Security is covered in the Privacy Policy.

The operator describes Road to Glory as a solo-developed indie web game intended for players worldwide and reports that the game currently has no license. Both drafts list `vdz.indie@gmail.com` for contact. Legal identity, country, age eligibility, data retention and deletion procedures remain unconfirmed.

The operator describes the game as a free, independent community project. Any future contributions are intended as voluntary support for development, with no in-game benefits. The terms describe the absence of affiliation, sponsorship, funding or endorsement by the football organizations and game publisher named there. Review third-party rights separately and verify any payment provider and its data flows before accepting contributions.

OpenFront’s [Privacy Policy](https://openfront.io/privacy-policy.html) and [Terms of Service](https://openfront.io/terms-of-service.html) informed the organization of the drafts, particularly service scope, information collected, how account access works and mandatory rights. The Road to Glory drafts describe the features and providers verified in this repository.

SynApp’s [Privacy Policy](https://synapp.vn/privacy) and [Terms of Service](https://synapp.vn/terms) were consulted for practical instructions on email requests and grouping the rules by topic. Road to Glory directs people to a single mailbox for requests about data, accounts and support.

## Legal review by market

- **Vietnam, privacy.** Law 91/2025/QH15 covers domestic operators and specified foreign processing involving Vietnamese citizens and certain other individuals. Article 4 covers access, correction, deletion, objection, withdrawal of consent and other data rights. Under Article 5 of Decree 356, qualifying rights requests generally require a response within two working days, with different fulfillment deadlines for each request type. Check processing and cross-border impact assessment obligations and exceptions separately under Articles 20–22 of the Law and the Decree. Document cloud and mailbox data flows before determining which filings are required. [Law 91/2025/QH15](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?docid=214590&pageid=27160), [Decree 356/2025/NĐ-CP](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?docid=216387&pageid=27160)
- **Vietnam, game release.** Article 37 of Decree 147 distinguishes G1 licensing and release approval from G2–G4 service certification and release confirmation. Classic and PvE use a server and saved accounts, so assess their actual player-to-server interactions as part of classification. Confirm the classification, operator eligibility and any required authorization before releasing in Vietnam. If the game falls within the regime, review age classifications, player information requirements under Article 56, applicable play limits and mandatory website disclosures. The current email and Google sign-in flows do not establish the identification measures described in the Decree. [Decree 147/2024/NĐ-CP](https://vbpl.moj.gov.vn/bovanhoathethao/Pages/vbpq-toanvan.aspx?ItemID=171689), [Ministry guidance on Articles 37 and 56](https://bvhttdl.gov.vn/tiep-nhan-va-tra-loi-y-kien)
- **Vietnam, consumer terms.** Determine whether the project is a covered supplier. If covered, preserve statutory complaint and remedy rights and review standard terms for prohibited clauses, including unilateral changes and restrictions on complaints. A support mailbox provides a contact channel. Applicable disclosures and complaint handling still need an operating process. [Consolidated Consumer Protection Law 47/VBHN-VPQH](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?docid=217232&pageid=27160)
- **EU, privacy and consumer terms.** Assess GDPR Article 3 based on establishment, offering services to people in the EU or monitoring their behavior there. If applicable, verify the notice, legal bases, request workflows and any international transfer mechanism under Articles 6, 12–22 and Chapter V. Separately review ePrivacy rules for analytics session storage under each relevant national law. If the project qualifies as a trader, national rules implementing Directive 2019/770 can cover some services supplied in exchange for personal data, subject to its limited-purpose exception. [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), [EDPB territorial guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/documents/guideline/guidelines-32018-on-the-territorial-scope-of-the-gdpr-article-3-version-adopted_en), [ePrivacy amendment](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2009/136/oj/eng), [Directive 2019/770](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/770/oj/eng)
- **UK and US, privacy.** Assess UK GDPR territorial scope and PECR separately. For PECR, assess both the analytics script and the session flags before enabling analytics, including whether an exception applies and which user choices it requires. In the US, the FTC explains that COPPA can apply to an overseas operator when a service is directed to children under 13 in the US or the operator knowingly collects their personal information. The applicable age and parental consent rules depend on audience and jurisdiction. [ICO territorial guidance](https://ico.org.uk/media2/migrated/4031113/ic-327905-y2y5-knowledge-hub-territorial-scope.pdf), [ICO PECR guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/how-do-we-comply-with-the-pecr-rules/), [FTC COPPA FAQs](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions)

## Open decisions before publication

- Confirm the legal operator and address, launch markets, game classification and age eligibility. Review any additional market requirements before actively offering the game there.
- Confirm who monitors the mailbox, how identity is verified, and how data requests, complaints and incident reports are handled. Confirm retention for support email, accounts, saves, backups, hosting logs and rate-limit records. The app currently has no verified self-serve account deletion workflow.
- Confirm processing purposes, legal bases, provider arrangements, deployment regions, transfer safeguards and any required user controls. [Supabase Auth password storage](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/password-security), [Vercel Web Analytics data handling](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy)
- Review rights to artwork, names and other third-party material, and any payment, donation or promotion before offering it. Set the effective date and review both translations before removing the draft notice and noindex directive.
